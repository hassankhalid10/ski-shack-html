About This Location

Your Pro Shop on the Water

Indian Point is our floating, on-the-water Pro Shop on Table Rock Lake. Pull up by boat to shop a full selection of watersports gear, try equipment before you buy with on-water demos, or rent gear for the day.

Our staff riders can help you pick the right gear for your skill level and boat. Need boat sales or service? Visit our Springfield showroom or the Mill Creek Service Center.

Proudly serving: Branson, Indian Point, Silver Dollar City area and Table Rock Lake.

FAQ

Indian Point Questions

Where is The Ski Shack Indian Point location?

Our Indian Point Pro Shop is at 3443 Indian Point Rd, Branson, MO 65616, near Table Rock Lake. Call 417.338.8481.

What does the Indian Point location offer?

Indian Point is a floating, on-the-water Pro Shop offering a full selection of watersports gear, equipment demos and watersports rentals. Boat sales are at our Springfield showroom, and service is at Springfield and Mill Creek.

Can I buy or service a boat at Indian Point?

Boat sales are handled at our Springfield showroom, and service is available at Springfield and the Mill Creek Service Center in Lampe.

Can I rent watersports gear at Indian Point?

Yes. Our Indian Point location offers water sport rentals for a day on Table Rock Lake. Call 417.338.8481 or send us a message to reserve. [Confirm rental items, rates and booking process with dealer.]

Can I demo gear before I buy?

Yes. Our Indian Point Pro Shop offers equipment demos right on the water, so you can try boards and gear before you buy.

Can I get to Indian Point by boat?

Yes. Indian Point is a floating, on-the-water Pro Shop, so you can pull right up by boat.

What are the Indian Point hours?

Indian Point is a seasonal location and is currently closed for the season. We typically open the weekend before Memorial Day — check back for our 2027 opening date.

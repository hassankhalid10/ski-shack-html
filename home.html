The Ski Shack is Southwest Missouri's destination for boats, full-service repair and maintenance. We're building a community of watersports enthusiasts and outdoor adventurers — and we stay with you long after the sale with maintenance, customization and real-world advice.

From Springfield to Table Rock Lake, our team serves boaters across Branson, Shell Knob, Nixa, Rogersville and the greater Ozarks.

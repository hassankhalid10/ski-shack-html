FAQ

Engine Repair Questions

What types of boat engines do you repair?

We service Malibu, PCM, Indmar, Volvo, Mercury and Yamaha engines, and we specialize in the service, maintenance and repair of inboard-powered boats.

Can you diagnose a check-engine light or warning code?

Yes. We use diagnostic equipment to read codes and pinpoint issues before recommending repairs.

Do you repair outboard motors?

Yes. We service and repair outboards, including those on pontoons and runabouts.

Will I get an estimate before work begins?

Yes. We review findings and pricing with you and get your approval before starting repairs.

How often should my boat engine be serviced?

Most engines need routine service every season or at the hour intervals in your owner’s manual. Our team can recommend a schedule for your engine.

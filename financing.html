FAQ

Boat Financing Questions

Does The Ski Shack offer boat financing?

Yes. We work with marine lenders to offer financing on new and pre-owned boats. You can start a pre-approval online or call our team at 417.882.8480.

How long can I finance a boat?

Boat loan terms often range from 10 to 20 years depending on the loan amount, the boat’s age and lender approval. Our finance team will review the options available to you.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Many marine lenders look for 10–20% down, but requirements vary by lender, credit profile and boat. A trade-in can count toward your down payment.

Can I finance a pre-owned boat?

Yes. Financing is available on many pre-owned boats. Terms may differ based on the boat’s age and value.

Can I use my trade-in toward my down payment?

Yes. Your trade-in value can be applied to your purchase to lower the amount you finance.

Will applying affect my credit score?

The pre-approval form starts the conversation with our team. Ask our finance team how and when a credit inquiry is made. [Confirm with lender.]

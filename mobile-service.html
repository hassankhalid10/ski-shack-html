FAQ

Mobile Service Questions

Where does The Ski Shack offer mobile boat service?

Mobile service is available on Table Rock Lake for boats purchased through The Ski Shack. Mobile service for boats purchased elsewhere may be available at our discretion — call 417.882.8480 to ask.

What can be done with mobile service?

Routine maintenance, diagnostics, battery and electrical work, impeller service and minor repairs can usually be handled dockside. Larger repairs are completed at our Springfield or Mill Creek service centers.

Do I need to be at the boat during service?

No. You don’t need to be present for your mobile service appointment — just make sure we have access to your boat.

Is there a fee for mobile service?

Mobile service may include a trip charge based on location. [Confirm pricing with dealer.]

What if my boat needs more work?

We’ll explain what we found and schedule your boat for service at Springfield or the Mill Creek Service Center.

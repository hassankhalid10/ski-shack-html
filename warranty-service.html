FAQ

Warranty Questions

Is The Ski Shack an authorized warranty service center?

Yes. The Ski Shack is the only dealership on Table Rock Lake factory-authorized to perform warranty work on Malibu, Axis, Nautique, Chris-Craft and Revel boats.

What should I have ready for a warranty claim?

Have your hull identification number (HIN), proof of purchase or registration, engine hours and a description of the issue.

What does my boat warranty cover?

Coverage varies by manufacturer, model year and component. Our service team can review your warranty and explain what’s covered.

Can you check if my boat has an open recall?

Yes. Give us your HIN and we’ll check for any open recalls or service campaigns.

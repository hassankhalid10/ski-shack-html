Our Story

More Than a Boat Dealer

At The Ski Shack, we’re not just about boats. We’re about building a community of watersports enthusiasts and outdoor adventurers who love spending their days on Missouri’s lakes.

Whether you’re shopping for your first pontoon or your next tournament-level wake boat, our team is here to help you find the right fit — and to keep supporting you long after the sale with maintenance, customization and advice.

About This Location

Service Right on Table Rock Lake

The Mill Creek Service Center on MO-13 in Lampe brings The Ski Shack’s factory-certified technicians close to the lake. We’re factory certified for Malibu, Axis, Nautique, Chris-Craft, Revel, PCM, Indmar, Yamaha and Mercury, and we service all boat brands.

Trouble on the water? Call 417.882.8480 and we’ll send someone out. There’s no problem our crew can’t solve.

Proudly serving: Lampe, Kimberling City, Shell Knob, Blue Eye and Table Rock Lake.

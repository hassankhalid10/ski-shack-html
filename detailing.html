FAQ

Detailing Questions

What boat detailing services does The Ski Shack offer?

We offer basic interior and exterior detailing, buffing and waxing packages, and ceramic coating services.

How much does boat detailing cost?

Pricing depends on the size of your boat, its condition and the services you choose. Request a quote and we’ll put together an estimate for your boat.

How often should I detail my boat?

Most boats benefit from a full detail at least once a season, with a wash and wax more often if your boat stays on the water.

Can you remove oxidation from my gelcoat?

Yes. Compounding and polishing can restore faded, chalky gelcoat and bring back the shine.

How long does a boat detail take?

It depends on your boat’s size, condition and package. We’ll give you an estimate when you schedule.

Should I detail my boat before selling or trading it in?

Yes. A professional detail helps your boat show at its best and can help support a stronger value.

FAQ

Trade-In & Brokerage Questions

What is Wake Brokers?

Wake Brokers is the dedicated brokerage division of The Ski Shack in Springfield, MO. We help buyers and sellers of wake and surf boats with appraisals, marketing, listings, showings, negotiations and finding hard-to-find boats.

How do I find out what my boat is worth?

Use the Start My Appraisal form on this page. We’ll prepare a market analysis based on comparable sales and current market trends.

Can Wake Brokers help me find a specific boat?

Yes. We search both on-market and off-market inventory through our industry connections, then help with inspections and guide you through the purchase.

Can I trade in my boat at The Ski Shack?

Yes. Submit your boat’s details online for an estimated trade-in value, then apply it toward a new or pre-owned boat from our inventory.

Should I trade in or sell through brokerage?

Trading in is the fastest, simplest option if you’re buying another boat. Brokerage can take longer but may bring a higher sale price. Our team can help you compare both.

Can I trade in a boat that still has a loan?

Yes. We accept trade-ins with existing loans. Any outstanding balance on your trade-in is paid off once financing for your new boat has been funded.

How is my boat’s value determined?

We look at the year, make, model, engine hours, condition, options and current market demand, then confirm the value with an in-person inspection.

FAQ

Boat Service Questions

Where is The Ski Shack’s service department?

Boat service is available at our Springfield location, 5539 S. Campbell, and at the Mill Creek Service Center, 8936 MO-13 in Lampe near Table Rock Lake. We also offer mobile service.

How do I schedule boat service?

Submit the service request form on this page or call 417.882.8480. Our service team will follow up to confirm your appointment.

What brands of boats and engines do you service?

We service all boat brands. Our technicians are factory certified for Malibu, Axis, Nautique, Chris-Craft, Revel, PCM, Indmar, Yamaha and Mercury.

What are your service hours?

Our Springfield service department is open Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM, and Saturday, 10 AM–2 PM. The Mill Creek Service Center is open Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM.

Can you help if my boat breaks down on the lake?

Yes. Call 417.882.8480 from Springfield or the Mill Creek Service Center in Lampe and we’ll send someone out to you.

How long will my boat be in the shop?

It depends on the work needed and the season. We’ll give you an estimated timeline when we confirm your appointment.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, scheduling ahead helps us get your boat in and out faster, especially during spring and fall rush.

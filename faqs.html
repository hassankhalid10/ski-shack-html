General

What boat brands does The Ski Shack sell?

The Ski Shack is an authorized dealer for Malibu, Axis, Nautique, Chris-Craft and Revel boats, plus a selection of quality pre-owned boats from other manufacturers.

Where are The Ski Shack locations?

Our main showroom is at 5539 S. Campbell in Springfield, MO. Our Indian Point Pro Shop is at 3443 Indian Point Rd in Branson, and our Mill Creek Service Center is at 8936 MO-13 in Lampe, near Table Rock Lake.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Springfield, Branson, Table Rock Lake, Shell Knob, Plano, Ebenezer, Rogersville, Nixa and the rest of Southwest Missouri.

How do I contact The Ski Shack?

Call 417.882.8480 for Springfield and Mill Creek, or 417.338.8481 for Indian Point. You can also send a message from our Contact page.

How quickly will The Ski Shack respond to my message?

Our team typically responds to all inquiries the same day. For immediate assistance, call us during business hours.

Sales

Do you sell used boats?

Yes. We carry inspected pre-owned boats and offer brokerage services if you want to sell your boat through us.

Can I order a boat that isn’t in stock?

Yes. Our sales team can help you build and order a new boat with the layout, options and colors you want.

Can I schedule a private showing?

Yes. Request a private showing online or call our Springfield showroom at 417.882.8480.

Financing

Does The Ski Shack offer boat financing?

Yes. We work with marine lenders to offer financing on new and pre-owned boats. You can start a pre-approval online or call our team at 417.882.8480.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Many marine lenders look for 10–20% down, but requirements vary by lender, credit profile and boat. A trade-in can count toward your down payment.

Service

Which location offers boat service?

Boat service and repair are available at our Springfield location and at the Mill Creek Service Center in Lampe.

What brands do you service?

We service all boat brands. Our technicians are factory certified for Malibu, Axis, Nautique, Chris-Craft, Revel, PCM, Indmar, Yamaha and Mercury.

What are your service hours?

Our Springfield service department is open Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM, and Saturday, 10 AM–2 PM. The Mill Creek Service Center is open Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM.

Brokerage & Trade-Ins

Can I trade in my boat?

Yes. Submit your boat online to get a trade-in value, then apply it toward a new or pre-owned boat.

What is Wake Brokers?

Wake Brokers is the dedicated brokerage division of The Ski Shack. We help buyers and sellers of wake and surf boats with appraisals, marketing, showings and negotiations.

Pro Shop

Where is the Pro Shop?

The Pro Shop is at our Springfield showroom and at Indian Point, our seasonal floating Pro Shop on Table Rock Lake.

What does the Pro Shop carry?

Wakeboards, wakesurf boards, life vests, ropes, apparel, accessories and lake-day gear from brands like Hyperlite, Liquid Force, Phase 5, HO, Follow, Connelly, Mission Boat Gear, Fat Sac and Seipel Marine.

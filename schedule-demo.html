FAQ

Private Showing Questions

Still have questions? Call 417.882.8480.

How do I schedule a private showing at The Ski Shack?

Fill out the request form on this page or call our Springfield showroom at 417.882.8480. A sales specialist will confirm the boat and your appointment time.

Where do private showings take place?

Showings take place at our Springfield showroom, 5539 S. Campbell, Springfield, MO 65810.

How long does a private showing take?

Most private showings last about an hour, but there’s no rush — take as much time as you need.

Can I bring my family or friends?

Yes. Let us know how many guests are coming so we can have the right boats ready to see.

Can I see more than one boat?

Absolutely. Tell us which models you’re comparing and we’ll have them ready so you can see them side by side.

FAQ

Hiring Questions

How do I apply for a job at The Ski Shack?

Fill out the application on this page or call 417.882.8480. You can apply for a specific opening or submit a general application.

Do I need boating experience to work at The Ski Shack?

Not for every role. A passion for boating and watersports helps, and we provide training for many positions. [Confirm with dealer.]

Do you hire seasonal employees?

Yes. We hire for seasonal roles, especially in the Pro Shop and service department during boating season. [Confirm with dealer.]

Where are the jobs located?

Openings may be at our Springfield showroom, our Indian Point Pro Shop in Branson or our Mill Creek Service Center in Lampe. Each listing shows its location.
